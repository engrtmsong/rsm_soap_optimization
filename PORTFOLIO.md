## Academic projects

### 1. Soap production optimization from *Balanites aegyptiaca* seed oil — B.Eng. research (2025)
- **Focus:** Non-edible feedstock utilization, experimental design, statistical optimization.
- **Methods:** Design of Experiments (DoE), Central Composite Design, Response Surface Methodology (RSM), Design-Expert.
- **Evidence:** Undergraduate research project described in academic CV.
- **Public code:** [Python RSM methodology demonstration](./README.md).
- **Data provenance:** The public Python demonstration uses representative/synthetic data, **not** the original laboratory measurements. Its predictions are not experimental thesis results.

### 2. Conceptual 100-tonne/day sodium bicarbonate plant design — B.Eng. design project (2025)
- **Focus:** Solvay-route process selection and conceptual plant design.
- **Scope in academic report:** Process flow diagram, material and energy balances, equipment sizing, process safety, instrumentation/control, environmental considerations, and economic evaluation.
- **Status:** Completed academic design report; no validated executable plant simulator or full public report is included here.
- **Future reproducible work:** A separately documented simulation model, design assumptions, and calculation checks, subject to data permissions.

### 3. Emulsion paint formulation from locally sourced raw materials — National Diploma project (2019)
- **Focus:** Locally available pigments, extenders, binders and additives; formulation and quality assessment.
- **Status:** Completed academic product-development project. No original formulation data or code is included in this repository.
