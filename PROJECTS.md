# Selected Chemical Engineering Projects

This page summarizes completed academic projects and future reproducible work. Full reports and proprietary data are not published here.

## 100-tonne/day sodium bicarbonate plant — 2025 academic design

**Institution:** Modibbo Adama University, Yola. **Status:** Completed conceptual academic design, not an operating plant or validated DWSIM model.

**Process:** Solvay-route production of sodium bicarbonate.

**Reported design scope:** process selection, process flow diagram, material and energy balances, equipment sizing, safety, instrumentation, environmental considerations and economic analysis. The original report is held privately. A public simulation model and independently verified results have **not** been uploaded.

**Potential next deliverables:** sanitized PFD, clearly sourced feed assumptions, a unit-by-unit balance workbook, equipment sizing calculations, and a validation report comparing hand calculations with simulation.
